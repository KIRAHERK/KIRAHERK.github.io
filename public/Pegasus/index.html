Pegasus plays the songs on your phone and any song online, with lyrics that light up word by word as they're sung. Open a track below to see it.

  1. An equalizer with presets, bass boost, crossfade between songs, even volume across songs, skip silence and a sleep timer. The player takes its colours from each cover, and you can pick red, green, blue, purple or pink for the rest of the app.

  2. Each word lifts and fills with light as the singer reaches it. When a song has no word timing, whole lines light up instead, and songs without timing still show their lyrics. Go full screen and the background moves with the cover's colours.

  3. Songs in Spanish, Korean, Japanese, French and more get an English line under each original line, slang included. One tap turns translations on or off. English and Arabic songs stay as they are.

  4. Search songs, albums, artists and playlists and play them straight away. Songs already on your phone come first, marked with a small phone.

    • Share a YouTube, YouTube Music or Spotify link to Pegasus and it plays.
    • Share a playlist link and it becomes your playlist.
    • Each song you start gets its proper name, artist and cover.

  5. Songs, albums, artists, folders, liked songs and history, all in one library. "Clean up my music" fixes names like "03 - Artist - Song (Official Video) [Lyrics]", finds proper covers and hides extra copies. You check every change first, your files stay untouched, and you can undo it all.

  6. Download one song or a whole album or playlist. It arrives in order, as its own playlist, saved as real music files in Music/Pegasus with name, artist and cover, so any music app can play them.

  7. Every month Pegasus puts together your top songs, artists and albums, the minutes you listened and your longest streak, as cards you tap through and share. It's worked out on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.
